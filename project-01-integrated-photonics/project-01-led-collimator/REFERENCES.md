# References

1. **Jiazhong Zeng, Xiufeng Li, and Peng Ge.** “Design of LED collimator for uniform illumination using two freeform lenses.” *Optica Applicata*, 48(3), 413–420 (2018). DOI: https://doi.org/10.5277/oa180307  
   Contribution: directly relevant two-freeform-lens LED collimator work and architectural context for uniform illumination.

2. **Emil Aslanov, Leonid L. Doskolovich, and Mikhail A. Moiseev.** “Thin LED collimator with free-form lens array for illumination applications.” *Applied Optics*, 51, 7200–7205 (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.51.007200  
   Contribution: demonstrates freeform lens-array approaches for LED collimation and illumination.

3. **Zheng Zhenrong, Hao Xiang, and Liu Xu.** “Freeform surface lens for LED uniform illumination.” *Applied Optics*, 48, 6627–6634 (2009). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.48.006627  
   Contribution: establishes freeform surface design as a method for redistributing LED emission toward uniform illumination.

4. **Yi Ding, Xu Liu, Zhen-rong Zheng, and Pei-fu Gu.** “Secondary optical design for LED illumination using freeform lens.” *Proceedings of SPIE*, 7103, 71030K (2008). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/12.797810  
   Contribution: provides secondary-optics/freeform-lens context showing the additional control available beyond conventional spherical surfaces.
